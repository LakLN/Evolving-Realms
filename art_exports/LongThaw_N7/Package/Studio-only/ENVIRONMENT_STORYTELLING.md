# Studio-only environmental storytelling constraints

This file contains spoiler-sensitive art direction. Keep it out of player-facing
summaries and contact-sheet labels.

- The nine weirs were built and repaired across generations. Tool marks, masonry and
  repair stakes should visibly disagree; no single uniform architectural kit should
  imply one builder or era.
- Weir Five's channel history reflects deliberate human choices with costs downstream,
  not supernatural climate machinery. Flood marks and maintenance evidence must remain
  readable without declaring one community purely guilty.
- Orchard damage is older flood/terrace damage. Sparse surviving growth is credible;
  a bustling settlement, fresh banners or universally abandoned ruin dressing is not.
- Siltmarket is a seasonal exchange ground. N6 contains no active NPC settlement state,
  so do not show occupied tents, lit braziers, fresh merchandise or residents.
- Alder/root repairs and mixed repair tags indicate practical cooperation. They are not
  faction heraldry.
- The three Weir Five outcomes change declared water cells and resource access only.
  Art must align to the supplied `open`, `brace` and `spill` masks and must not invent
  additional collapsed structures, new routes or displaced buildings.
- Creature homes and conditioned arrivals are Run-local gameplay. Environment art may
  show habitat evidence, but must not bake a living creature into ground or prop art.
