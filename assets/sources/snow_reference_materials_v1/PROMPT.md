## Exact generation prompt

Create a production texture atlas for an original classic top-down pixel-art winter fantasy RPG, NOT a concept scene. Exactly 4 columns and 2 rows, 8 equally sized square texture cells, no gutters, no labels, no borders, no icons or objects, no text. Each cell is a flat orthographic material sample, lit softly from upper left, crisp restrained hand-pixel clusters, NOT photoreal, no gradients, no perspective, no bevel frames. Row 1 left to right: (1) quiet powder snow in ivory with subtle blue-grey wind-swept pixel clusters, very sparse texture, (2) compacted snowy earth path in subdued taupe grey with small irregular embedded stones, not cobblestone pavement, (3) vertically exposed cold blue-grey mountain rock strata with dark cracks and occasional snow in crevices, (4) weathered warm brown horizontal timber planks with sparse nail details. Row 2 left to right: (5) snow-covered slate roof texture, overlapping irregular blue-grey shingles capped with soft white snow, (6) rough ancient grey granite masonry with sparse green-grey lichen, not perfectly regular bricks, (7) dark deep cold water with sparse subtle blue horizontal ripples, (8) old timber bridge deck viewed straight overhead, tightly packed vertical boards, naturally weathered, no rails or framing. Cohesive muted mountain palette across the atlas. Every square is edge-to-edge material suitable for cutting into repeatable game tiles, no baked cast shadows from off-image objects. Aim for rich controlled pixel craftsmanship like original 16/32-bit adventure RPG environments while keeping snow intentionally restful and low contrast. Final atlas 1536 by 768 pixels if possible, equal 384px squares; clear exact 4x2 arrangement is mandatory.
