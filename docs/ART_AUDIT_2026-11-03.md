## Consistency findings

- **Pixel density / native size:** all runtime outputs are downscaled AI boards with
  hard 0/255 alpha (props shadow 70, translucent overlays are the intended exceptions).
  Density is broadly consistent because one build pipeline produced them.
- **Proportion:** body heights vs the 47 px hero: wolf 1.15, deer 1.09, boar 0.79,
  crab 0.70, rabbit 0.60, **S1 snow wolf 0.74** (35 px). The snow manifest asks 44-52 px
  for the scree wolf, so the S1 frames cannot be reused at spec size without rescaling.
- **Outline:** Batch01/02 sprites carry a dark outline; S1 snow wolf and S1 props are
  soft and unoutlined. In `snow-scree-wolf.png` the white wolf separates from taiga
  mainly through the code hostile ring.
- **Perspective/light:** three-quarter top-down throughout; mirrored side rows (player
  attack, boar all states, wolf death, snow wolf all) flip the upper-left light.
- **Feet and pivots:** no idle/walk frame of any sheet is more than 4 px from its anchor
  and no foot-line jitter was found: the existing foot anchoring works.
- **Directions/frames:** all 7 sheets expose 20 animations; the snow wolf fills 32 slots
  with 19 unique frames (5 reused sequences).
- **Terrain:** variants join exactly (shared edge bands); `snow-arrival.png` and
  `snow-scree-wolf.png` show a visible 40 px repeat on taiga.
- **Import:** every indexed texture is lossless, mipmap-free, alpha-carrying and the
  indexed size; Godot re-saves `.import` files with CRLF only (no parameter change).

## Cosmetic backlog (logged, not fixed)
